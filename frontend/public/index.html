Step 3 of 5: Upload or take crop photos

Standardized photos allow experts to evaluate the condition accurately without repeated follow-up calls. Follow the visual guidance below:

Photo 1 — Whole Plant

Step back 1–2 meters. Capture the entire plant from soil level to canopy.

Photo 2 — Affected Area

Hold camera 30–50 cm away. Focus on the infected branch or clustered leaves.

Photo 3 — Close-up Detail (Optional)

Clear close-up (10–15 cm) showing spot texture, margins, or vein discolorations.

Privacy Protection: Photographs should focus exclusively on crops, leaves, stems, and soil. Never photograph people, faces, vehicles, or personal documents.

Review 1 requires demonstrating at least three specific edge and failure cases. Click below to execute and inspect the system response for each scenario:

1. Poor Image Quality

Tests behavior when a farmer uploads a blurry, severely underexposed, or low-contrast leaf photo. Shows non-blocking guidance.

2. Low Confidence (<60%)

Tests behavior when the vision model confidence drops below 60%. Automatically promotes case to High Priority triage.

3. Conflicting Override

Tests behavior when expert overrides an AI proposal. Expert diagnosis is strictly authoritative; AI saved for error audit.

4. Offline Queue & Sync

Simulates farmer submitting while offline. Stores safely in IndexedDB, then triggers auto-sync to backend when connected.

5. Duplicate Prevention

Tests idempotent client-sync tracking. Prevents duplicated case submissions caused by network retries or repeated sync calls.

6. Missing Environment

Tests submission without environmental or microclimate indicators. Validates graceful defaulting to "Unknown" without failure.

7. Tracking & Resubmit
